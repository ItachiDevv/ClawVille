feat(casino): Phase 6.1.7 — slot modal layout overhaul + cylinder drums

Cherry-charm-inspired redesign. Reels now dominate the modal (~70% of
viewport) and render as 3D rotating drums instead of flat planes.

Layout (SlotScreenModal.tsx + SlotHUD.tsx):
  - Modal maxWidth 720→1200.
  - SlotHUD gains `section?: 'top' | 'bottom'` prop; undefined renders
    both sections (backwards-compat). Modal mounts two instances:
    section="top" above reels (balance / session / spins strip),
    section="bottom" below reels (predict chips + SPIN + walk-away).
  - Reel area = `flex:1 minHeight:50vh` flexbox child between the two
    HUD strips. SlotReelsCanvas container 100%×100% (was fixed
    480×360). At 1280×800 viewport the reels canvas now occupies
    ~55-60% of modal viewport area, up from 22%.
  - "PREDICTIVE GAMING COVE" subtitle text deleted (redundant with the
    header chip; was stealing vertical space).

Cylinder drums (SlotReels3D.tsx — full rewrite):
  - CYLINDER_RADIUS = (STRIP_LEN × CELL_WU) / 2π ≈ 13.37 wu. Derived
    from the wrap math: 84 cells × 1.0wu = circumference, so radius is
    determined.
  - REEL_SPACING = 27.5 wu (was 1.3 in be14553 — that was the geometric
    bug that made all 5 drums overlap into a single tangled mass).
    Now 2×radius + 0.76 gap.
  - CylinderGeometry(13.37, 13.37, 3, 84, 3, true). Open-ended (no
    end caps).
  - Spin animation rotates mesh around Y-axis; texture is static and
    UV-wraps around the cylinder circumference via wrapS=RepeatWrapping.
  - 4-phase physics preserved (ACCEL 200ms / STEADY 1.5s / DECEL 600ms
    staggered / POP 120ms).
  - Texture relayout: 84 columns × 3 rows on the canvas (was 1 column ×
    84 rows in the planar version). Cell (k, row) at texture coord
    (k × TILE_PX, row × TILE_PX). UV mapping: theta=0..2π → u=0..1;
    y=-h/2..+h/2 → v=0..1 split into 3 rows.
  - Cell backgrounds changed from rgba(17,32,61,0.88) dark slate to
    #6b3aa0 Tyrian purple with rounded corners — matches cherry-charm
    aesthetic.
  - White bezel rings: TorusGeometry(13.52, 0.12, 8, 84) at y=±1.5wu
    on each drum, rotated π/2 to lie horizontal. Two per drum, 10 total
    bezels. MeshBasicMaterial white.
  - Deadlock fix from Phase 6.1.6 preserved: useEffect([reels]) has NO
    isSpinning guard. `anim.targetSet` flag inside the loop prevents
    double-computation; `setDisplayWindow(spinResult.reels)` from
    SlotScreenModal lands reels mid-animation.

Canvas (SlotReelsCanvas.tsx):
  - camera position [0, 0, 120], fov 60° (was z=5, fov 65°).
  - 5 drums × 27.5 wu = 137.5 wu wide. Viewport at z=0 with fov 60°
    from z=120 = 2 × 120 × tan(30°) = 138.6 wu. Fits with 1.1 wu margin.
  - frameloop="always" + preserveDrawingBuffer:true preserved from
    Phase 6.1.6 (don't regress 812fea9 / d7949ca).
  - DPR cap [0.55, 0.7] on Iris Xe baseline.

The cylinder concept was right in be14553; only the spacing+camera
were wrong. Two refactor rounds (cylinder→plane→cylinder) confirmed
the planar approach was a dead end for the cherry-charm visual target.
