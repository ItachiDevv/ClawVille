Asset pipeline:
  - 7 source 1024×1024 PNGs in ~/Downloads/clawville-new (3D-rendered
    chibi characters + lobster + pixel-art coin).
  - .tmp/optimize-slot-icons.mjs runs a 4-step sharp pipeline:
      1. Knock near-white background → alpha 0..255 ramp (threshold
         230..250 to soften edges, no hard mask jaggies).
      2. Trim transparent edges (threshold 10 to clear the alpha-ramp
         tail).
      3. Extend → centered square with 6% breathing-room padding.
      4. Resize to 512×512 (lanczos3) + PNG palette encode (192 colours,
         quality 92).
  - File-size reduction: ~89-95% (source ~700 KB-1 MB → output 21-89 KB).
  - Total roster GPU footprint: ~376 KB across 7 PNGs. Well inside the
    ~2 MB texture budget the SlotReels3D CanvasTexture cells consume.

Manifest updates:
  - packages/shared/src/constants/slot-symbols.ts —
    CLASSIC_SLOT_SYMBOL_ASSETS now points at the new .png paths with new
    displayName + themeColor per character. `svgPath` field name kept
    (carries .png paths now — renaming would ripple through 6+ consumer
    files for label change with no behavioural value).
  - packages/shared/src/constants/slot-paytables.ts —
    CLASSIC_SYMBOLS + BONUS_SYMBOLS keep id/payouts/wild/scatter

    change.
