feat(casino): Phase 6.1.5 — Bundle B bonus mechanics (scatter + free spins + multiplier wilds)

New classic-3x5-bonus paytable additive to classic-3x5. Engine,
routes, frontend, verifier, sim, CI gate, and orientation knowledge
all updated in one slice.

Engine (apps/api/src/services/slot-engine.ts):
  - Scatter symbol (id 10 Treasure Chest) pays anywhere, not
    payline-restricted: 3 anywhere = 2x predict, 4 = 10x, 5 = 50x.
  - Scatter does NOT participate in payline matching.
  - Multiplier wilds: each landed Wild (id 7) gets one extra
    sampleIntFromBytes(range=100) draw — 0-59 -> 2x, 60-89 -> 3x,
    90-99 -> 5x. Cursor advances 4 bytes per draw.
  - Multiple wilds on a winning line: multipliers MULTIPLY.
  - Free-spin trigger: 3+ scatters in base mode -> 10 free spins.
    Retrigger: 3+ scatters in FS -> +5, capped 50 total.
  - SpinResult extended with wildMultipliers + scatterPayout +
    freeSpinsAwarded + isFreeSpin (all now live).
  - RunSpinArgs gains freeSpinMode?: boolean.

RTP-shape lock (option (a) — orchestrator decision 2026-05-19):
  Free-spin mode does NOT double line wins OR multiplier values.
  Constants: FS_LINE_WIN_MULTIPLIER=1, FS_WILD_MULTIPLIER_DOUBLE=false.
  Value to player = free spins themselves are free (zero predict
  debit). Spec-literal interpretation produced 129% combined RTP —
  unshippable. With option (a): 96.02% classic / 97.29-97.57%
  combined bonus on 100k Monte Carlo.
