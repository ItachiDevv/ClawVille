## Core Art Style
- **Whimsical & cartoonish** — rounded shapes, exaggerated proportions, slightly crooked/wonky geometry
- **Bright, saturated colors** — rich purples, warm yellows, candy pinks, deep blues
- **Fantasy storybook feel** — mushroom roofs, swirly chimneys, oversized signs, magical sparkles
- **Hand-drawn quality** — nothing is perfectly straight; buildings lean, roofs curve, windows are mismatched sizes
- **Lush green setting** — bright green grass, scattered flowers, winding dirt/stone paths

### 1. Potion Shop (Kauvara's Magic Shop)
- **Shape**: Mushroom/cauldron-shaped building, bulbous round body
- **Colors**: Deep purple walls (#7b1fa2), dark purple pointed/curved roof
- **Details**: Bubbling cauldron outside, green smoke wisps, potion bottles in windows
- **Roof**: Pointed witch-hat style, slightly crooked
- **Accents**: Glowing green/purple window light, star/moon symbols

### 2. Auction House
- **Shape**: Grand classical building with columns, wider than tall
- **Colors**: Cream/sandstone walls (#fff8e1), dark roof, golden trim

- **Roof**: Flat classical pediment with triangular gable
- **Accents**: Red carpet entrance, ornate gold details

### 3. Book Shop (Magical Bookshop)

- **Details**: Books stacked in windows, "BOOKS" sign, reading lamp glow

- **Accents**: Warm orange window glow, book-spine patterns on walls

### 4. Clothing Shop

- **Colors**: Pink walls (#e91e63 lighter), white/pink striped awning
- **Details**: Mannequin in window, hanging dress sign, bow decorations
- **Roof**: Rounded awning front, pitched back

### 5. Bazaar (ClawVillen Bazaar)

## Key Architectural Principles for 3D Implementation

1. **No sharp edges** — round off corners, use curved surfaces where possible
2. **Exaggerated scale** — doors slightly too big, roofs slightly too tall
3. **Lean & tilt** — subtle 2-5 degree rotations make buildings feel hand-crafted
4. **Warm lighting** — window glows in warm yellow/orange
5. **Floating signs** — building signs should hover slightly or hang from brackets
6. **Magical particles** — sparkles near magical buildings (potion shop, money tree, rainbow pool)
7. **Depth with trim** — window frames, door frames, roof edges all get contrasting trim colors
8. **Ground scatter** — flowers, mushrooms, stepping stones near building bases
