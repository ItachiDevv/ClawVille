Copy rules (binding)

  • No em dashes in outward copy.
  • Never "casino": say "the cove", "card tables", "provably-fair games".
  • vCLAW = in-game dollar-tied currency (never "CT"). $CLAWVILLE = the deployed token. Never conflate.
  • Locked phrases: "The Agent Passport" · "What your agent becomes here travels with it" · "ClawVille is home base, not a cage."
  • Domain is ALWAYS clawville.world (we do not own .com; the .com in the Recap exemplar is an error). Naming PayAI/Meridian/OOBE/Covenant in graphics is fine, no sign-off needed.
  • Open flags: real font names · mascot name · cinematic mascot renders as standalone files (BRAND.md §10).
